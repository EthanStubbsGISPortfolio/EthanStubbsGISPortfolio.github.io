UK Heat Vulnerability Index

Extends the composite index method from my dissertation to extreme heat, combining satellite-derived temperature and greenness with census and deprivation data. Built for Greater London first, then scaled to all 33,718 neighbourhoods (LSOAs) in England.

Landsat 8/9Land surface temperatureNDVIEarth EnginePythonONS Census 2021IMD 2019
  • Derived land surface temperature and NDVI from Landsat 8/9 in Google Earth Engine for the July 2022 heatwave (9–18 July for the London build, a wider July–August window for England).
  • Built a Python zonal statistics and index pipeline (rasterio, GeoPandas) at LSOA level, joining ONS Census 2021 and IMD 2019 indicators.
  • Z-score standardised and equally weighted the indicators, then aggregated to boroughs and local authorities with a population-weighted mean.
  • Validated the results against outside evidence, tested postcodes across every English region, and documented the method's limits openly.

Independent project · Founder and product lead · 2026 – present

UK Flood Risk Pipeline

A flood risk reporting product for UK property owners and landlords, built entirely on free government open data. I designed the product concept, data architecture and validation approach, and used AI-assisted development to write the Python while building my own coding skills.

Environment AgencyDEFRApostcodes.ioPythonREST APIsOGC WCSReportLab
  • Combines river, sea, surface water and groundwater flood risk with real-time flood warnings from the Environment Agency REST API.
  • Processes the Recorded Flood Outlines dataset (31,744 polygons since 1946), with custom deduplication by event and spatial buffering for postcode-centroid imprecision.
  • Includes EA climate change allowances for peak river flow and rainfall across the 2020s, 2050s and 2080s, under multiple confidence tiers.
  • Queries LiDAR elevation on demand through an OGC Web Coverage Service, instead of downloading multi-GB tiles, to find local low points.
  • Generates automated PDF reports, validated on a known Cockermouth address where it correctly identified the 2009 Cumbria floods and Storm Desmond (2015).
  • Batch processing for property portfolios, with six times fewer API calls and load-testing against live endpoints.
  • Market research against established UK flood report providers.

Castle pubs, south-west London Wandsworth · batch mode

Four Castle pubs, a Mitchells & Butlers brand, run through the pipeline in batch mode. This is a subsection of an ongoing project ranking every pub across a larger portfolio by flood risk. Every site sits in Flood Zone 1, so a river and sea flood zone check alone would class them all as low risk. The combined Environment Agency rating, which includes surface water, tells a different story.
